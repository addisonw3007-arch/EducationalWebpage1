All About Cats

Cats are one of the most popular pets in the world.

What Makes Cats Unique?

Cats are known for being independent, curious, and intelligent animals. Domestic cats can have very different personalities, with some being extremely social and others preferring to spend more time alone.

Common Cat Characteristics

  • Excellent hearing
  • Strong night vision
  • Flexible bodies
  • Highly developed sense of balance
